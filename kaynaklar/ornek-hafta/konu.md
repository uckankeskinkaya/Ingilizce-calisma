# Week 0 (Sample): Present Perfect vs. Past Simple

## 1. Present Perfect Tense

**Form:** have / has + past participle (V3)

- I **have visited** London.
- She **has finished** her homework.
- They **haven't seen** the new film yet.
- **Have** you ever **eaten** sushi?

**We use the Present Perfect:**

1. For experiences in our life (when is not important): *I have been to Italy twice.*
2. For actions that started in the past and continue now (with *for* and *since*):
   *We have lived here for ten years. / We have lived here since 2015.*
3. For recent actions with a result now: *I have lost my keys.* (I can't open the door now.)
4. With *just, already, yet*: *He has just left. / I have already eaten. / Have you finished yet?*

## 2. Past Simple Tense

**Form:** verb + -ed (regular) or V2 (irregular)

- I **visited** London **last year**.
- She **finished** her homework **two hours ago**.
- **Did** you **see** the film **yesterday**?

**We use the Past Simple** for finished actions at a specific time in the past.
Time expressions: *yesterday, last week, in 2019, two days ago, when I was a child.*

## 3. Present Perfect or Past Simple?

| Present Perfect | Past Simple |
|---|---|
| Time is not stated / not finished | Specific finished time |
| *I have seen that film.* | *I saw that film last Friday.* |
| *She has worked here since May.* (still works) | *She worked here in 2020.* (doesn't work now) |
| ever, never, just, already, yet, for, since | yesterday, ago, last ..., in 2010 |

**Common mistake:** ~~I have seen him yesterday.~~ → *I saw him yesterday.*

## 4. Key Vocabulary

- **experience** – deneyim
- **recently** – son zamanlarda
- **already** – zaten, çoktan
- **yet** – henüz (olumsuz/soru)
- **ever** – hiç (soru)
- **never** – asla, hiç
- **since** – -den beri (başlangıç noktası)
- **for** – boyunca (süre)
